# Viewpoints.ai

> Viewpoints.ai runs AI jury simulations for litigation teams and
> AI audience panels for market researchers. Upload case materials;
> a representative panel of simulated jurors reads them, deliberates,
> and returns the verdict distribution, the themes driving it, and
> juror-by-juror reasoning. Change the venue or the opening and run
> it again the same afternoon. SOC 2 Type II. Patented.

## Products

- [AI Jury Simulation](https://viewpoints.ai/jury-sim): simulated mock
  juries for trial teams, insurers, and general counsel. Themes,
  verdict distributions, deliberation dynamics, venue-matched panels.
- [AI Market Research](https://viewpoints.ai/market-research):
  synthetic personas for concept, message, and creative testing.

## Evidence

- [Human vs. machine mock trial (DRI 2026)](https://viewpoints.ai/viewpoints-dri-for-the-defense.pdf):
  live head-to-head; the simulated panel matched the human mock jury's
  defense verdict and surfaced the same leading themes.
